## 2. Rules derived from those lines

1. **Start where the reader is.** Name their current situation in their words before offering the next step.
2. **"You" and "I".** Direct address; first person singular for his own view and work.
3. **Short declaratives.** Many sentences under 12 words. A two word fragment can land a point ("No code.").
4. **Time frames make it real.** "In an afternoon", "two weeks", "every two weeks". Prefer a concrete duration to "fast".
5. **Real work, not toy examples.** "A real problem in your work", "on its own backlog".
6. **Honest about the hard part.** Building is easy now; judging whether it is good is the hard part. Admit doubt where it is real.
7. **Few contractions.** The site copy writes "you are", "I am", "it is". Default to that in written posts unless his posts show otherwise. `[NEEDS DEJAN INPUT: confirm]`
8. **Plain language promise.** No code assumed, no jargon without a one line explanation.
9. **Outcome over activity.** Packages and courses are described by what you leave with.

Open question for Dejan: his site uses the contrast pattern "A, not B" ("In an afternoon, not a quarter"), while his `strategic-deck` skill bans "X, not Y" rhetoric in decks. Decide whether the pattern is allowed in posts, and how often. `[NEEDS DEJAN INPUT]`
